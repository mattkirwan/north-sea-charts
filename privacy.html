Privacy

North Sea Surf keeps anonymous usage stats to see how the site is used and make it better.

They count page visits, the first thing done on the chart (moving it, zooming, using the timeline or changing layer), whether the site is open in a browser, in Instagram or from the home screen, and the link that brought you here. They come with a random ID made on this device and stored only on it, so a return visit counts as the same visitor. It isn't tied to your name, email or anything else about you.

Feedback sent from the menu comes to me by email: what you write, with the app's language, its version and whether it's open in a browser, in Instagram or from the home screen. Nothing else about you, unless you put it in the message.

There are no ads and no profiling, and nothing is shared or sold. The stats are kept with Umami. Browsers sending Do Not Track are not counted.
